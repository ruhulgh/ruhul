Research Interests

VValue Chain Analysis

Mapping and optimizing agricultural value chains to improve efficiency, reduce post-harvest losses, and enhance smallholder participation in profitable markets.

PPolicy Analysis

Evaluating agricultural policies and their impacts on farm households, market systems, and national food security outcomes in Bangladesh.

CClimate Adaptation

Studying climate-smart agricultural practices and adaptation strategies for vulnerable farming communities in southern Bangladesh.

FFood Security

Assessing food and nutrition security indicators, linking agricultural production systems to dietary diversity and health outcomes.

Research Projects

1 Value Chain Analysis of Rice in Southern Bangladesh

Ongoing

A comprehensive study mapping the rice value chain from production to consumption in Gazipur and surrounding districts. The project identifies bottlenecks, price transmission gaps, and opportunities for smallholder integration into premium markets.

Duration: 2023 – Present  |  Role: Principal Investigator  |  Funding: UGC Bangladesh

2 Climate Resilience of Coastal Agribusiness Systems

Completed

An interdisciplinary project examining how coastal agribusinesses adapt to salinity intrusion, cyclone risk, and market disruptions. Findings informed regional adaptation planning documents.

Duration: 2020 – 2023  |  Role: Co-PI  |  Funding: World Bank Sub-project

3 Smallholder Market Integration in Vegetable Production

Completed

Investigated market participation determinants among smallholder vegetable farmers in south-western Bangladesh, examining contract farming potential and collective marketing approaches.

Duration: 2018 – 2021  |  Role: PI  |  Funding: NATP-II, BARI

A About Me

Md. Ruhul Amin is a Assistant Professor in the Department of Agribusiness at Gazipur Agricultural University, Bangladesh. With extensive experience in agricultural economics and agribusiness management, he has dedicated his career to advancing understanding of value chains, rural livelihoods, and sustainable agricultural development in Bangladesh.

His research focuses on the intersection of agribusiness competitiveness, smallholder market integration, and climate-resilient farming systems. He has contributed significantly to policy dialogues on food security and agricultural transformation in South Asia.

"Sustainable agribusiness is not just about profitability — it's about building resilient food systems that serve both farmers and consumers in an era of climate uncertainty."

Prior to joining GAU, Md. Amin served in various academic and research capacities, contributing to national and international agricultural development projects. He actively supervises graduate research and collaborates with international research institutions.

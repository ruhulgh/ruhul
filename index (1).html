About Me

I am an enthusiast in the field of Agricultural Economics, highly motivated to transform academic knowledge into practical output through field-level research on Agriculture Market Research, Consumer Research, Energy and Environment, and Food Security. My work blends research, teaching, and field engagement to address real-world challenges in food systems, energy use, and environmental resilience.

Driven by the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), I explore how rural communities interact with markets, adopt clean technologies, and adapt to climate change. From consumer research to groundwater irrigation, my projects aim to create practical impact in policy and practice. I enjoy mentoring students and collaborating across disciplines to shape a more inclusive and sustainable agribusiness future.

I have taught in the Department of Agribusiness at Gazipur Agricultural University since December 2020, and in 2026 I began a PhD at the School of Education and Professional Studies, Griffith University (Nathan Campus), Australia.
